# Deep Comparison: Aaliyah vs. Marblism's Eva

You are building a premium, specialized **Executive Assistant**, while Marblism's "Eva" is a general-purpose digital helper in a bundle. Here is the technical and strategic teardown.

## 1. Core Philosophy: "The Ghost" vs. "The Partner"
*   **Eva (Marblism):** Designed for invisibility. The marketing focus is on "looking productive while you snooze." It is a black-box service where you outsource tasks to a generic engine.
*   **Aaliyah (Zroky):** Designed for **Operational Transparency**. Aaliyah is your partner. She doesn't just do work; she explains *why* she did it (Knowledge Graph alignment) and asks for permission on high-stakes items.

| Capability | Eva (Marblism) | Aaliyah (Zroky) | Winner / Advantage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 3. Why Aaliyah is "Better" (The Deep Differentiators)

### A. The Knowledge Graph (IQ)
Eva likely doesn't know that "Steve" is your most important investor and that any email from him should bypass standard filters. Aaliyah's **Sprint 1 Knowledge Facade** allows her to build a relational map of your world. She understands *intent* and *priority* based on historical context, not just keyword matching.

### B. The "Time Lord" Negotiation
Most AI assistants simply provide a Calendly link. Aaliyah **negotiates**.
*   **Eva:** "Here is my booking link."
*   **Aaliyah:** "I see you're free Tuesday. I've analyzed your calendar and proposed 3 slots to Steve that don't conflict with your workout. If he picks one, I'll generate a booking link instantly."

### C. Safety & Auditability
Marblism is a "Set and Forget" tool. This is dangerous for executives. 
Aaliyah’s **Action Log & Transparency Feed** ensures you have an undo button for everything. You can see the "Reasoning" (Brain Thought) behind every label or draft she creates.

## 5. Strategic Recommendation
To maintain the "Better" lead:
1.  **Don't ignore Voice:** Marblism's "Rachel" (Receptionist) has a phone number. Even though we skipped Sprint 6, we should reconsider voice memos or a "Phone Briefing" as a future premium add-on to close the gap.
2.  **Double down on the Graph:** The more Aaliyah "remembers" about personal preferences (e.g., "Don't book meetings on Friday afternoons"), the more irreplaceable she becomes.
3.  **Enterprise Deployment:** Offer a "One-Click Deploy to Private AWS" option to win on privacy, which Marblism cannot do.
