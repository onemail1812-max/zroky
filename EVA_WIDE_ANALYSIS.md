# Deep Analysis: Marblism Eva vs. ZROKY Aaliyah

## Part 1: Marblism Eva - Wide Feature & Workflow Analysis

Marblism’s **Eva** is positioned as a turn-key "AI Employee" specialized for Executive Assistant duties. It aims to bridge the gap between simple chatbots and human employees by integrating directly into the user's primary communication tools.

### 1. Onboarding & Setup Workflow
The "Zero to Hero" path for Eva is optimized for speed, promising a fully functional assistant in under 60 minutes.

*   **Credential Integration (The OAuth Handshake):**
    *   Setup begins with connecting **Gmail or Outlook** and **Google/Microsoft Calendars**.
    *   Eva also supports standard **IMAP** for legacy email providers, a key differentiator for professional flexibility.
*   **Persona Calibration:** 
    *   Instead of just choosing a "tone" (like Professional/Casual), Eva requests **examples of previous emails**. She scans hundreds of sent messages to "ingest" the user’s unique voice, sentence structure, and typical sign-offs.
*   **Taxonomy Configuration:**
    *   The user defines high-level "Buckets." By default, Eva suggests: *To Respond, FYI, Marketing, and Newsletters.*
    *   Unlike static filters, Eva's taxonomy is dynamic; she can create her own sub-categories (e.g., "Investor Relations") as she detects patterns in the data.
*   **Workspace Assignment:**
    *   For power users, Marblism allows "One Eva per Inbox," meaning a user can have a specific EA persona for their work email and another for their personal or venture email.

### 2. Core Operational Capabilities
Eva operates as a background process that "augments" the existing inbox rather than replacing it with a new app.

*   **Intelligent Triage:**
    *   **The Shadow Inbox:** Eva organizes the incoming flood into logical priorities. She is known for her ability to "summarize" threads, so an executive can see a 20-email chain condensed into three bullet points.
*   **Personalized Drafting (Ghostwriting):**
    *   Eva doesn't just draft; she "learns." She places her responses in the **Drafts folder** of the native email app (Gmail/Outlook). 
    *   She is specifically tuned to handle "No-Brainer" emails: introductions, "thank you" notes, and basic scheduling queries.
*   **Calendar & Time Governance:**
    *   **Conflict Avoidance:** She doesn't just check for free slots; she proactively flags when a meeting is being booked too close to another or during a user's "quiet hours."
    *   **Meeting Prep:** Before a call, Eva generates a "Briefing Note" containing the LinkedIn profiles of attendees and the last three interactions with that contact.
*   **Meeting Transcription & Action items:**
    *   Eva joins Zoom/Teams/Google Meet as a silent observer, transcribing the call and automatically creating action items in a linked task manager (like Notion or Slack).

### 3. User Experience (The "Agentic" Feel)
*   **Interaction Model:** Most interactions happen inside the Inbox or via Slack/Email. There is a web dashboard for "Management," but the goal is to never have to visit it.
*   **Learning Loop:** Eva has a "Correction Mechanism." If a user edits a draft she created, she analyzes the delta to refine her future drafts.

## Part 2: Head-to-Head Comparison: ZROKY Aaliyah vs. Marblism Eva

While Eva is a polished general-market tool, **Aaliyah** is engineered for a higher tier of **Operational Sovereignty** and **Deep Strategy**.

| Feature Category | **Marblism Eva** | **ZROKY Aaliyah** | **The Winning Difference** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Philosophy** | **Outsourcing:** Aimed at the "Invisible Assistant" who hides complexity. | **Partnership:** Aimed at the "Co-Decision Maker" who adds layers of IQ and strategy. | Aaliyah is for those who lead; Eva is for those who want to hide. |
| **Intelligence Root** | **Style Analysis:** Uses LLM context and pattern matching from sent mail. | **Memory Architect:** Uses a dedicated **Knowledge Graph** to map intents and social graphs. | Aaliyah doesn't just mimic your voice; she understands your **goals**. |
| **Scheduling IQ** | **Proactive Admin:** Flags conflicts and manages time zones. | **The Time Lord:** Uses complex logic to protect "Energy Blocks" and handle multi-party negotiation skills. | Aaliyah treats your time as a finite asset to be **negotiated**, not just filled. |
| **Transparency** | **Black Box:** You see the result (the draft). The logic is hidden. | **Clear Box:** The **Intelligence Feed** shows her internal "Brain Thoughts" and reasoning for every action. | Executives value **Explainability** for high-stakes labeling and triage. |
| **Safety Logic** | **Continuous Learning:** Relies on the user editing drafts over time. | **Review-First Autonomy:** Built-in QA triggers and hard guardrails for sensitive topics. | Aaliyah is architected to **Never Fail Silently**. |
| **Deployment** | **Multi-tenant SaaS:** Your data is on Marblism’s cloud. | **Private HQ:** Fully Dockerized. Can live on your own infrastructure or Private Cloud. | **Sovereignty.** In 2026, privacy is the ultimate executive luxury. |

### Summary for ZROKY Roadmap
Aaliyah distinguishes herself by being **Integrated Logic**, not just **Integrated Text**. 

While Eva can write a "thank you" email better than Aaliyah's current v1.0, Aaliyah will eventually be able to say: *"I didn't just draft this email for Steve; I drafted it because he is 3 weeks away from a Series A decision, and I've highlighted the personal connection we made in 2025."*

**Verdict:** Eva is the competitor to beat on **Polish**, but Aaliyah has already won on **Architecture**. Our focus for Sprint 7+ should be on hardening that "Sovereign AI" feel that a public SaaS platform cannot offer.
